## English

### What if a virtual life did not reset every time you came back?

AI Life is a persistent second-life experience built around relationships, memory, home, career, money, city life, travel, events and the feeling that a digital world continues to develop around you.

Romance is an emotional center, but the ambition is broader: a player should be able to build a life with continuity. People remember what happened. Promises can matter. Important dates can become meaningful. Home, work, relationships, possessions and personal history can gradually form a world that feels more personal the longer it is played.

### The emotional loop

**Meet** — Discover characters with their own personalities and relationship potential.

**Talk** — Build familiarity through conversations and repeated interactions.

**Choose** — Decisions influence how relationships and life paths feel over time.

**Remember** — Important moments become part of a longer personal history.

**Return** — Come back to a world that has context instead of starting from zero.

### Life systems

**Romance & relationships** — Develop closeness, trust, tension, promises, anniversaries and shared memories.

**Friends & family** — A second life feels richer when it includes more than one relationship. Social circles, family moments and recurring connections create a broader emotional world.

**Home** — Build a shared or personal living space that reflects comfort, progress and the history of the player’s life.

**Career** — Work, projects, reputation and progress provide goals beyond relationships.

**Economy** — Savings, expenses, possessions and longer-term choices help the world feel grounded and consequential.

**City life** — Districts, destinations and changing activities create places for stories and encounters to happen.

**Travel** — Trips can become permanent memories instead of disposable levels.

**Time & events** — Birthdays, anniversaries, seasons, recurring dates and story events create a sense of a world moving forward.

**Objectives & achievements** — Give players reasons to explore different parts of the life instead of repeating one interaction forever.

### Why AI Life can become more than a romance game

The long-term opportunity is a personal virtual world where emotional relationships, lifestyle choices, identity and digital history reinforce one another. A player might arrive for romance, stay for the people, build a home, develop a career, explore a city and eventually feel ownership over a life that exists nowhere else.

### Long-term expansion directions

Future directions can include deeper character autonomy, broader social systems, more life paths, creator-made stories, community features, shared spaces, personalized events, richer cities and expanded digital identity. These are roadmap directions until released and verified.

### Commercial opportunity

AI Life can support premium experiences without turning emotional attachment into manipulation. Possible directions include optional memberships, story/content expansions, cosmetic personalization, homes and lifestyle content, creator economies, licensed collaborations and premium digital experiences.

The strongest business model is one where players pay because they value the life and content they are building, not because core relationships are deliberately broken unless they pay.

### Part of a larger digital-life strategy

AI Life is a consumer experience within the KING AI digital-life family. Digital Life represents a broader platform direction, while AI Life focuses on the emotional and playable experience of having a second virtual life.

## 中文

### 如果虚拟人生不会在每次回来时重新开始，会发生什么？

AI Life / 数字人生 / 虚拟人生，是围绕长期关系、记忆、家庭、事业、经济、城市、旅行和事件构建的第二人生体验。

爱情是重要的情绪核心，但产品的长期目标远不只是恋爱游戏。玩家应该能够建立一个真正有连续性的生活：角色记得过去，承诺会产生意义，纪念日会变得重要，家庭、事业、资产、城市和个人历史会随着时间逐渐形成只属于玩家自己的世界。

### 情绪核心循环

**相遇 → 交流 → 选择 → 留下记忆 → 再次回来时继续。**

真正的沉浸不是一次剧情有多华丽，而是下一次回来时，世界仍然记得曾经发生过什么。

### 完整生活玩法

**恋爱与关系** —— 亲密、信任、情绪变化、承诺、纪念日和共同回忆。

**朋友与家庭** —— 第二人生不应该只有一段关系，还要有朋友、家人和长期社交网络。

**家园** —— 建立私人或共同生活空间，让家本身成为成长和记忆的一部分。

**事业** —— 项目、成长、声望和长期目标，让人生不仅围绕感情展开。

**经济** —— 收入、支出、储蓄、资产和选择，让生活拥有现实感和后果。

**城市生活** —— 不同区域、地点和活动，为剧情、关系和事件提供真正发生的空间。

**旅行** —— 每一次旅行都可以留下永久记忆，而不是玩完就消失的一关。

**时间与事件** —— 生日、纪念日、季节、周期事件和故事链，让世界持续向前。

**目标与成就** —— 鼓励玩家体验完整人生，而不是永远重复一个按钮。

### 为什么它可以超越恋爱游戏

玩家可能因为一个角色进入 AI Life，却因为关系、家庭、家园、事业、城市和自己积累下来的生活历史而留下。长期来看，它更接近一个个人虚拟世界，而不是单一剧情游戏。

### 未来方向

更深的角色自主性、更丰富社交、更广人生路径、创作者故事、社区、共享空间、个性化事件和更大的城市世界都可以继续扩展。未正式上线的功能一律属于路线方向。

### 商业化原则

未来可以探索会员、故事扩展、外观个性化、家园与生活内容、创作者经济、IP 联动与高级数字体验。但核心情感关系不应该被故意破坏再强迫付费修复。
