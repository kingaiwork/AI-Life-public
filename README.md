# English

## What if your virtual life remembered the life you actually built?

Most games are built around levels, matches or short sessions. Most AI experiences reset context quickly. AI Life explores a different kind of attachment:

**a life you return to because the people, places, choices and history are yours.**

Romance can be an emotional center, but the product is designed to become much broader than a romance game. A persistent second life becomes interesting when the player can build relationships, make commitments, develop a career, manage money, create a home, explore a city, travel, experience time passing and accumulate a personal history that becomes harder to replace the longer it grows.

## The emotional promise

The strongest moment in a persistent-life game is not necessarily the first meeting.

It is the moment you return later and the world still remembers:

- who you care about;
- what happened before;
- what promises were made;
- what changed while your life developed;
- which home you built;
- what career path you chose;
- what milestones became part of your story.

That continuity is what can turn a short experience into a long-term relationship with the product.

## Core gameplay loop

**Meet → Talk → Choose → Build → Grow → Remember → Return.**

### Meet
Discover characters, places and opportunities that can become part of your life.

### Talk
Build familiarity through repeated conversations and interactions rather than one disposable scene.

### Choose
Relationships, career, money and lifestyle decisions shape how the life feels over time.

### Build
Create a home, social circle, personal routine and sense of ownership over the world.

### Grow
Develop relationships, career progress, assets, goals and identity.

### Remember
Important moments become part of an accumulating personal history.

### Return
Come back to a world with context instead of restarting from zero.

## Major life systems

### Romance and relationships
Closeness, trust, tension, promises, anniversaries, conflict, recovery and shared memories can give relationships emotional depth over time.

### Friends and family
A believable second life needs more than one relationship. Friends, relatives and recurring social connections make the world feel broader and less scripted around one person.

### Home and lifestyle
A personal or shared home can become a visible expression of progress, comfort, identity and relationship history.

### Career and ambition
Jobs, projects, reputation and long-term goals create purpose outside romance and give the player different ways to define success.

### Economy and possessions
Income, expenses, savings, assets and lifestyle choices help decisions feel consequential instead of purely cosmetic.

### City life
Districts, destinations, activities and recurring locations give relationships and events a physical sense of place.

### Travel
Trips can become memorable chapters in a life rather than disposable stages.

### Time and events
Birthdays, anniversaries, seasons, recurring dates and special events help the world feel like it is actually moving forward.

### Goals and achievements
Long-term objectives encourage the player to experience more of the world instead of repeating the same interaction forever.

## Daily, weekly and long-term reasons to return

### Daily
Check in with important relationships, handle life decisions, explore changing opportunities and make small progress.

### Weekly
Pursue larger goals, strengthen relationships, develop career or home progress and experience new events.

### Long-term
Build a recognizable life story, achieve milestones, create deeper relationships, accumulate assets and unlock new life paths.

A strong life game should create a reason to return without making the player feel punished for stepping away.

## Why AI can make a life game feel different

The long-term opportunity is not simply “AI characters that can chat.” The more meaningful direction is characters and situations that feel less disposable because the experience can respond to history, relationships and changing context.

Product directions can include:

- richer character personality;
- deeper long-term relationship continuity;
- more personal events;
- broader life choices;
- more varied responses to past decisions;
- evolving social networks;
- more dynamic city experiences;
- creator-made stories and worlds;
- shared or community experiences where appropriate.

These remain roadmap directions until released and verified.

## Why players may stay

- emotional attachment can deepen over time;
- a personal history becomes difficult to replace;
- multiple life systems create more than one reason to play;
- home, career and economy add ownership beyond relationships;
- city and travel create discovery;
- time-based events create anticipation;
- future creator and social systems can expand the world continuously.

## Commercial opportunity

AI Life has room for premium entertainment without making emotional attachment exploitative.

Potential models include:

- optional memberships;
- story and world expansions;
- cosmetic personalization;
- homes, interiors and lifestyle content;
- premium events;
- creator economies;
- licensed collaborations;
- premium digital-life experiences.

A healthy model should make players want to pay because they value the world they are building — not because core emotional relationships are deliberately broken unless they pay.

# 中文

## 如果虚拟人生真的记得你曾经建立的一切，会发生什么？

大多数游戏围绕关卡、对战和短时间体验展开；很多 AI 每次回来也像重新认识你一次。

AI Life 想做的是另一种长期吸引力：

**玩家回来，是因为这里的人、关系、家、选择和人生历史已经真正属于自己。**

爱情可以是最重要的情绪入口之一，但长期方向远不只是恋爱游戏。一个真正有吸引力的第二人生，还应该包括朋友、家庭、家园、事业、经济、城市、旅行、时间、事件和长期个人历史。

## 真正让人沉浸的，不只是第一次相遇

最有力量的时刻，可能是你几天以后、几周以后再次回来，世界依然记得：

- 你在乎谁；
- 以前发生了什么；
- 你们做过什么承诺；
- 哪些关系发生了变化；
- 你建立了怎样的家；
- 你选择了怎样的事业；
- 哪些重要日子已经成为你的人生历史。

这种连续性，才有机会把一次体验变成长期关系。

## 核心玩法循环

**相遇 → 交流 → 选择 → 建立生活 → 成长 → 留下记忆 → 再次回来继续。**

### 相遇
认识可能进入你人生的角色、地点和机会。

### 交流
关系通过长期互动逐渐熟悉，而不是只存在于一次剧情。

### 选择
感情、事业、金钱和生活方式决定，会持续改变人生方向。

### 建立生活
拥有家、社交圈、日常习惯和真正属于自己的世界。

### 成长
关系、事业、资产、目标和身份不断积累。

### 留下记忆
重要时刻进入长期历史，而不是剧情播放完就消失。

### 再次回来
下一次进入时继续自己的人生，而不是从零开始。

## 完整生活系统

### 恋爱与关系
亲密、信任、矛盾、承诺、纪念日、修复和共同回忆，让关系随着时间产生深度。

### 朋友与家庭
真正的第二人生不应该只围绕一个角色。朋友、家人和长期社交网络让世界更完整。

### 家园与生活方式
私人或共同的家可以成为成长、审美、关系和人生进度的可见表达。

### 事业与目标
工作、项目、声望和长期目标，让玩家可以用不同方式定义“人生成功”。

### 经济与资产
收入、支出、储蓄、资产和生活选择，让决定产生真实后果。

### 城市生活
不同区域、地点和活动，为关系、故事和随机事件提供真正发生的空间。

### 旅行
旅行不是一次性关卡，而可以成为人生历史里的一个章节。

### 时间与事件
生日、纪念日、季节、周期日期和特殊事件，让世界拥有时间流动感。

### 目标与成就
长期目标鼓励玩家体验完整人生，而不是永远重复一个互动。

## 每天、每周、长期都为什么回来

### 每天
看看重要的人、处理生活选择、发现变化、推进一点点人生进度。

### 每周
完成更大的目标、发展关系、事业或家园，体验新的事件。

### 长期
形成真正独特的人生历史，达到里程碑，建立更深关系，积累资产并开启更多人生路线。

一个好的虚拟人生应该让玩家愿意回来，而不是因为离开几天就被惩罚。

## AI 能让虚拟人生有什么不同

真正有价值的方向，不只是“NPC 会聊天”，而是角色和世界能够因为长期关系、过去历史和变化中的背景而越来越有个人感。

长期可以扩展更丰富角色个性、更深关系连续性、更个人化事件、更广生活路线、更明显的历史影响、更复杂社交网络、更动态城市、创作者故事和合适的共享体验。

这些未上线能力仍然属于路线方向。

## 为什么玩家可能长期留下

- 感情会随着时间加深；
- 自己的人生历史越来越难被替代；
- 多个生活系统提供不同玩法目标；
- 家园、事业和经济增加真正的所有感；
- 城市与旅行带来探索；
- 时间事件制造期待；
- 未来创作者和社交能力可以不断扩大世界。

## 商业化空间

未来可以探索可选会员、故事扩展、外观个性化、家园与生活内容、高级事件、创作者经济、授权联动和高级数字人生体验。

健康的商业模式应该让玩家因为“珍惜自己正在建设的人生”而愿意付费，而不是故意破坏核心情感关系再强迫付费修复。
