# AI Life · 数字人生 / 虚拟人生

## English

**Your second digital life — a persistent virtual world in the KING AI ecosystem.**

AI Life explores a different kind of consumer experience: not a disposable chat session and not a game world that emotionally resets every time it opens, but a digital life that can preserve relationships, places, goals, events, possessions and memories over time.

### The experience

AI Life is being developed around:

- persistent characters and relationships;
- romance, friendship and family storylines;
- a shared digital home and evolving environments;
- career, projects, reputation and life progression;
- cities, travel, seasons and recurring events;
- collections, achievements, objectives and personal history;
- long-term memory and return-time world continuity;
- future richer AI-driven characters, personalized stories, creator content and social worlds when supporting systems are mature.

### Why users may care

Most digital experiences forget the user as soon as a session ends. AI Life is built around the opposite idea:

**The world should remember that you were there.**

That creates room for stronger emotional attachment, longer-term engagement and a world that becomes more personal over time.

### Part of KING AI

AI Life is a core KING AI ecosystem product and the consumer entertainment expression of the ecosystem's memory, identity and continuity direction.

# 中文

**AI Life / 数字人生 / 虚拟人生** 是 KING AI 主生态的消费者数字生命与虚拟世界产品。

它不是一次性的 AI 对话，也不是每次打开都像重新开始的普通游戏，而是希望形成一个能够长期延续的“第二人生”：关系会成长，世界会变化，用户会拥有家、事业、旅行、资产、事件和长期记忆。

### 产品体验

AI Life 正在围绕以下方向发展：

- 持久角色与长期关系；
- 恋爱、友情和家庭故事；
- 共享数字家庭与持续变化的环境；
- 事业、项目、声誉与人生成长；
- 城市、旅行、季节和周期事件；
- 收藏、成就、目标与个人历史；
- 长期记忆和离线后继续发展的世界；
- 在基础系统成熟后扩展更丰富的 AI 角色、个性化剧情、创作者内容和社交世界。

AI Life 最重要的理念是：**这个世界应该记得你来过。**

AI Life 属于 KING AI 主生态核心产品。未来能力会明确作为路线方向展示，不把尚未完成的功能描述为已经全面上线。
