## English

### What if a virtual life did not reset every time you came back?

AI Life is a persistent second-life experience built around relationships, memory, home, career, money, city life, travel, time, events and the feeling that a digital world can become more personal the longer you live inside it.

Romance can be an emotional center, but the ambition is broader: a player should be able to build a life with continuity. People remember. Promises matter. Important dates return. A home changes. Career choices accumulate. Travel leaves memories. The world should feel increasingly like **your life**, not a sequence of disposable scenes.

### Why this kind of game can feel different

Traditional content often ends when the scene ends. AI Life is designed around accumulation:

- relationships grow from repeated interaction;
- important events become part of a personal history;
- home and possessions reflect long-term progress;
- career and economy create goals outside romance;
- city and travel create places connected to memories;
- time gives birthdays, anniversaries and recurring events meaning;
- choices become more valuable when the world remembers them.

### The emotional loop

**Meet → talk → choose → build trust → share experiences → create memories → return later and continue.**

The most important retention idea is simple: the player should want to come back because something meaningful is already waiting there.

### The everyday life loop

A normal return can include:

- checking how relationships have changed;
- continuing conversations or promises;
- managing home and personal goals;
- progressing career or projects;
- handling money, expenses or savings choices;
- exploring a district or destination;
- participating in events;
- reviewing objectives, memories and achievements.

The experience should offer enough variety that “living” feels broader than repeating the same interaction.

### The long-term loop

Over weeks and months, the player can build:

**Relationships** — Familiarity, trust, closeness, conflict, promises and shared history.

**Family & social life** — Friends, family and recurring connections beyond one romance path.

**Home** — A personal or shared living space that reflects the story of the life being built.

**Career** — Projects, growth, reputation and longer-term ambition.

**Economy** — Savings, expenses, assets and choices that create consequence.

**City life** — Districts, activities and recurring places that become familiar.

**Travel** — Trips that leave memories rather than disappearing like one-time levels.

**Time** — Birthdays, anniversaries, seasons, recurring events and milestones.

**Achievements & identity** — A growing record of what kind of life the player has chosen to build.

### Who it is for

- players who enjoy relationship and life simulation;
- people who prefer emotional continuity over short disposable sessions;
- fans of interactive storytelling;
- players who enjoy home, career, economy and city systems alongside relationships;
- users interested in persistent digital companionship and identity.

### What success should feel like

The player should not merely remember the game. They should remember **their version of the life**: who they became close to, where they lived, what they promised, what changed, what they built and what they still want to do next.

### Future expansion directions

These remain roadmap directions until released and verified.

### Responsible commercial principle

Potential future value can include memberships, story expansions, lifestyle content, home personalization, creator content, licensed collaborations and premium digital-life experiences.

## 中文

### 如果虚拟人生不会在每次回来时重新开始，会发生什么？

AI Life / 数字人生 / 虚拟人生，是围绕长期关系、记忆、家庭、事业、经济、城市、旅行、时间和事件构建的第二人生体验。

爱情可以是重要情绪核心，但长期目标远不只是恋爱游戏。角色应该记得过去，承诺应该产生意义，纪念日应该再次到来，家会变化，事业会累积，旅行会留下回忆，最终形成一种“这是我自己的第二人生”的感觉。

### 为什么它会和普通剧情游戏不同

普通内容往往一段剧情结束就归零，而 AI Life 强调累积：

关系通过长期互动变化；重要事件形成个人历史；家园和资产反映成长；事业和经济提供感情以外目标；城市和旅行变成熟悉地点；时间让生日、纪念日和季节真正有意义。

### 情绪循环

**相遇 → 交流 → 选择 → 建立信任 → 共同经历 → 留下记忆 → 下次回来继续。**

真正的留存不是“每天签到”，而是玩家回来时，已经有值得继续的人和生活在等待。

### 日常玩法

查看关系变化、继续对话和承诺、经营家园、推进事业、管理经济、探索城市、旅行、参加事件、完成目标、回顾记忆和成就。

### 长期玩法

**关系、朋友与家庭、家园、事业、经济、城市、旅行、时间、事件、成就、个人身份**共同组成一段长期生活，而不是单一按钮循环。

### 理想体验

玩家离开以后记得的不是“这个游戏有几个功能”，而是：**我和谁建立了关系、住在哪里、做过什么选择、留下了哪些回忆、下一次回来还想继续什么。**

### 未来扩展

更深角色自主性、更大关系网络、更多职业和人生路径、更丰富城市与旅行、长剧情链、创作者故事、社区/共享空间、个性化事件和更长期数字身份。未正式发布的能力继续标注为路线图。

### 商业化原则

应该让玩家因为喜欢故事、生活和个性化内容而愿意付费，而不是故意破坏核心情感关系再强迫付费恢复。

### 与 Pet / Digital Life 的关系

AI Life 是第二人生体验；Pet 是数字宠物陪伴与冒险；Digital Life 是更大的长期身份、记忆和连续性方向。
