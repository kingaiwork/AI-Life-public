# AI Life · Public Roadmap

## English

### Now
- improve relationship, home, career, city, travel and event continuity;
- strengthen performance, onboarding and return-time world updates;
- expand long-term memory and personal-history visibility;
- improve mobile-first UI and accessibility.

### Next
- richer character identity and memory;
- more dynamic relationship and life-event systems;
- additional cities, travel, careers and story chains;
- deeper personalization and world progression.

### Longer term
- more capable AI-driven characters;
- creator-made stories and world content;
- social and shared-world experiences where safety systems support them;
- cross-device continuity, digital possessions and broader entertainment/IP opportunities.

Roadmap items are directional until explicitly released and verified.

# 中文

### 当前重点
- 完善关系、家庭、事业、城市、旅行和事件的连续性；
- 提升性能、新手体验和离线返回后的世界更新；
- 强化长期记忆和个人历史展示；
- 优化移动端优先 UI 与可用性。

### 下一阶段
- 更丰富的角色身份与记忆；
- 更动态的关系与人生事件系统；
- 增加城市、旅行、事业与多阶段剧情；
- 更深入的个性化和世界成长。

### 长期方向
- 更强的 AI 驱动角色；
- 创作者制作的剧情和世界内容；
- 在安全体系成熟后扩展社交与共享世界；
- 跨设备连续体验、数字物品和更广泛的娱乐/IP 商业机会。

路线图内容在正式发布和验证前均属于方向，不代表已经全面可用。
