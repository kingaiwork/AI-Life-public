How it plays

A life loop, not a level list.

AI Life is designed around continuity. Every return can reveal something new: a relationship moment, a career opportunity, a world event, a bill, a trip, a memory or a consequence from an earlier choice.

World systems

The world should feel alive between clicks.

Time · Weather · Seasons · Events · Economy · Home · Career · Relationships · Travel · Collections

AI Life combines emotional systems and game systems so the world can generate reasons to return without turning daily play into chores. The goal is curiosity and attachment — not friction.

Story chains

A small choice can become a later invitation, career opportunity, conflict, promise, new location or permanent memory.

Offline continuity

Return-time catch-up can let the world advance fairly while the player is away, without punishing normal life outside the game.

Identity

Career, home style, relationships, travel history, collections and future customization make the second life increasingly personal.

Long-term opportunity

From life simulation to a digital-life platform.

As the supporting systems mature, AI Life can expand toward memory-aware AI characters, richer dynamic stories, creator-made content, optional social worlds, cross-device continuity and entertainment/IP collaborations. These are roadmap directions, not claims of current universal availability.

简体中文

一个会记得你的第二人生。

AI Life / 数字人生 / 虚拟人生不是一次性的聊天，也不是每次打开都重新开始的普通游戏。你可以建立关系、拥有自己的家、发展事业、探索城市、旅行、积累资产与记忆，并在下一次回来时看到这个世界仍然延续。

关系会留下历史

约会、承诺、生日、纪念日、冲突与和解都可以成为长期记忆，而不是结束后消失的单次剧情。

世界会继续变化

时间、天气、季节、事件、事业与离线补算，让玩家回来时面对的是继续发展的生活。

越玩越像“我的人生”

家庭、事业、旅行、收藏、关系和个人历史共同形成长期数字身份。

玩法不是只有聊天

生活目标、事业项目、城市探索、经济选择、家庭升级、旅行和多阶段剧情形成完整循环。

未来更聪明的角色

路线方向包括长期记忆对话、稳定性格、情境反应、更动态故事以及安全可控的生成体验。

创作者与商业空间

未来可扩展创作者剧情、地点、时装、季节内容、IP 联动与可选高级体验。
