# AI Life · Gameplay, World & Long-Term Experience

## English

AI Life is designed as a **persistent second-life game**. The goal is not to win one match or clear one fixed story. The goal is to build a life that keeps gaining history.

> **Return to a world that remembers you.**

## Core gameplay loop

```text
Return to the world
      ↓
See what changed while you were away
      ↓
Talk · choose · work · explore · care · travel
      ↓
Relationships, mood, career, money and world state change
      ↓
Memories and personal history are created
      ↓
New events, opportunities and consequences appear
      ↓
Return later to a world that remembers
```

This makes AI Life closer to **living inside an evolving story** than completing a fixed sequence of levels.

## 1. Relationship gameplay

Relationships are one of the emotional centers of AI Life.

Players can build long-term bonds through:

- conversations and meaningful choices;
- dates and shared activities;
- promises and follow-through;
- birthdays, anniversaries and important dates;
- trust, closeness, mood and attention;
- memories of earlier interactions;
- missed commitments and consequences;
- changing friendships and family relationships.

The long-term goal is for relationships to feel less like a progress bar and more like a **shared history**.

A relationship can become warmer, distant, complicated or deeply trusted based on what happened before. Important moments can become part of the memory timeline instead of disappearing after one scene.

## 2. A home that feels like a place

The digital home is intended to become a persistent personal space rather than a menu screen.

Gameplay can include:

- room interactions;
- comfort and atmosphere;
- home upgrades;
- shared moments with characters;
- possessions and decorations;
- location-linked memories;
- collections and future customization.

The emotional goal is simple: opening the game should feel like **returning somewhere**.

## 3. Career and personal growth

AI Life is broader than romance and conversation. It is also a life-progression game.

Career gameplay can include:

- projects and milestones;
- inspiration and productivity;
- reputation;
- recurring income;
- choices that trade time, money and relationships;
- future branching career paths;
- special opportunities triggered by previous decisions.

This creates a world where progress in one area can affect another. A career opportunity may create income but cost time. A relationship decision may unlock a different life path. A strong reputation may change what events appear.

## 4. Economy and everyday choices

A believable life world needs consequences.

The economy direction can include:

- income;
- savings;
- recurring expenses;
- bills;
- purchases and home upgrades;
- travel costs;
- inventory and consumable items;
- long-term goals and lifestyle choices.

The economy is not intended to turn the game into accounting. It exists to make choices, tradeoffs and progress feel meaningful.

## 5. City exploration

AI Life is designed as more than a room-and-chat experience.

The world can expand through districts and locations with different moods, activities and story opportunities. World concepts include places such as:

- **Star Bay** — calmer social and relationship atmosphere;
- **Cloud Studio** — creativity, work and inspiration;
- **Neon District** — nightlife, energy and unexpected events;
- **Travel Port** — departures, destinations and long-distance memories;
- expandable districts and future destination areas.

Locations can support dates, career opportunities, purchases, events, memories and multi-step story chains.

## 6. Travel that becomes permanent history

Travel is designed to create experiences that remain part of the player's story.

The direction includes:

- destinations;
- travel stamps;
- permanent travel memories;
- location-specific events;
- relationship moments tied to trips;
- rare or seasonal storylines;
- future collections and destination unlocks.

A trip should not disappear when the player returns home. It should remain part of the life history.

## 7. Time, weather and seasons

The world is designed to have its own sense of time.

That can include:

- world-day progression;
- weather;
- time-of-day phases;
- seasons;
- birthdays and anniversaries;
- recurring calendar events;
- limited opportunities;
- return-time catch-up after the player has been away.

Continuity matters because a world feels more alive when it is not frozen until the next click.

## 8. Story chains instead of isolated pop-ups

Events can connect into multi-step storylines rather than appearing as unrelated notifications.

A choice today can lead to:

- a future invitation;
- a relationship change;
- a career opportunity;
- a conflict;
- a promise;
- a new location;
- a rare item;
- a permanent memory.

The longer-term direction is a world where story grows from both designed content and the player's own history.

## 9. Daily play without turning life into chores

AI Life can support lightweight daily reasons to return:

- check what happened while away;
- respond to a relationship moment;
- complete a small life objective;
- progress a career project;
- explore a city event;
- improve the home;
- prepare for a trip;
- collect a new memory or achievement.

The design goal is to avoid a checklist that feels like work. Daily systems should create curiosity, emotional continuity and optional progression.

## 10. Objectives, achievements and collections

AI Life needs emotional goals and game goals.

Progression can include:

- daily objectives;
- life milestones;
- achievements;
- inventory collections;
- travel stamps;
- relationship memories;
- career milestones;
- home upgrades;
- hidden or rare events;
- long-term completion goals.

Players should be able to look back and see not only a level number, but **the life they built**.

## 11. Long-term memory timeline

A major differentiator is the idea that meaningful history should persist.

A memory timeline can record:

- first meetings;
- important conversations;
- dates;
- promises;
- conflicts and reconciliation;
- birthdays;
- trips;
- achievements;
- major purchases;
- career milestones;
- life-changing events.

Over time, the history itself can become one of the most valuable parts of the experience.

## 12. Offline continuity

The world should not feel frozen simply because the player closed the app.

The product direction includes return-time catch-up and continuing-world simulation so that players can come back to a world that has advanced rather than one that waited motionless.

This should remain fair: the world can move without punishing players simply for being away.

## 13. Character personality and future AI direction

As supporting systems mature, AI-driven characters can become richer through:

- stronger personality consistency;
- memory-aware dialogue;
- context-aware reactions;
- relationship-specific behavior;
- changing preferences and routines;
- more dynamic story generation;
- safer and more controllable generative experiences.

The opportunity is not to make characters random. It is to make them **feel more coherent because they remember context**.

These are roadmap directions until released and verified.

## 14. Player identity

A second life becomes more interesting when the player also has a persistent identity.

Longer-term systems can support:

- personal profile and life history;
- preferred lifestyle choices;
- relationship identity;
- career identity;
- home style;
- collections and achievements;
- travel history;
- future appearance/customization choices.

The player's identity should become increasingly personal rather than resetting to a generic protagonist.

## 15. Future social world

When safety and moderation systems are ready, AI Life can expand toward:

- social visits;
- shared locations;
- community events;
- collaborative experiences;
- friend/world discovery;
- optional shared memories;
- community-created spaces.

Social systems should be additive rather than mandatory. The personal second-life experience remains valuable even for players who prefer to play alone.

## 16. Creator ecosystem direction

A large persistent world can eventually become a platform for creators.

Potential creator opportunities include:

- authored storylines;
- designed locations;
- fashion and decoration packs;
- character concepts;
- event concepts;
- destination content;
- creator-curated life experiences.

The long-term opportunity is to let creators expand the world without exposing private engine or security implementation.

## 17. Business model possibilities

AI Life can support multiple non-exclusive commercial directions as the product matures:

- free entry with a large accessible core;
- optional premium stories and destinations;
- seasonal experiences;
- cosmetic personalization;
- home and lifestyle content;
- optional subscriptions for richer ongoing experiences;
- creator content revenue sharing;
- character/IP collaborations;
- entertainment partnerships;
- future digital-life platform services.

These are strategy directions, not current revenue claims.

## 18. Why the experience can retain players

AI Life combines several retention loops:

- **emotional loop** — relationships and memories;
- **progression loop** — career, home, economy and achievements;
- **discovery loop** — new places, events and story chains;
- **collection loop** — items, stamps, milestones and memories;
- **continuity loop** — the world changes while time passes;
- **identity loop** — the player's life becomes increasingly personal;
- **anticipation loop** — unfinished promises, upcoming events and future consequences.

The aim is not to trap players with friction. It is to create a world that feels worth returning to.

## 19. What makes AI Life different

AI Life is not positioned as only:

- a chatbot;
- a dating simulator;
- a clicker game;
- a static visual novel;
- a traditional life-sim clone.

Its product thesis is the combination of:

**persistent relationships + long-term memory + evolving world + life progression + player history.**

That combination is the foundation for a more personal digital life.

# 中文

AI Life 的核心不是“打一局赢一次”，而是建立一个**会持续积累历史的第二人生**。

> **下一次回来，这个世界还记得你。**

## 核心玩法循环

```text
重新回到世界
      ↓
看看离线期间发生了什么
      ↓
聊天 · 选择 · 工作 · 探索 · 陪伴 · 旅行
      ↓
关系、心情、事业、金钱和世界状态发生变化
      ↓
形成新的记忆与人生历史
      ↓
触发新的事件、机会和后果
      ↓
下一次回来，世界仍然记得
```

AI Life 希望让玩家感觉自己是在**生活在一个不断发展的故事里**，而不是完成一串固定关卡。

## 1. 会真正变化的关系

关系是 AI Life 最重要的情感核心之一。

玩家可以通过以下方式建立长期关系：

- 对话和有意义的选择；
- 约会和共同活动；
- 承诺与兑现；
- 生日、纪念日和重要日期；
- 信任、亲密、情绪与关注；
- 过去互动形成的长期记忆；
- 失约后的真实后果；
- 朋友与家庭关系的持续变化。

长期目标不是只看一条“好感度进度条”，而是让关系真正拥有**共同历史**。

## 2. 一个真正属于你的家

数字家庭不应该只是菜单背景，而应该成为玩家长期返回的个人空间。

玩法包括：

- 房间互动；
- 舒适度与氛围；
- 家庭升级；
- 与角色共同发生的生活片段；
- 家具、物品与收藏；
- 与地点绑定的长期记忆；
- 未来更丰富的个性化装修与展示。

目标是让玩家打开游戏时有一种：**“我回家了”** 的感觉。

## 3. 事业与个人成长

AI Life 不只做恋爱和聊天，还希望形成更完整的人生模拟。

事业玩法可以包含：

- 项目和里程碑；
- 灵感与效率；
- 声誉；
- 持续收入；
- 时间、金钱与关系之间的取舍；
- 未来更多分支职业；
- 由过去选择触发的特殊机会。

事业可能带来收入，也可能占用时间；关系可能影响人生选择；声誉可能改变未来事件——这让不同系统真正互相影响。

## 4. 金钱与生活选择

一个可信的人生世界需要“选择有代价”。

经济玩法方向包括：

- 收入；
- 储蓄；
- 固定支出；
- 账单；
- 购买与家庭升级；
- 旅行费用；
- 物品与消耗品；
- 长期目标和生活方式选择。

经济系统不是为了让玩家做会计，而是让决定、取舍和成长更有意义。

## 5. 城市探索

AI Life 不希望只停留在一个聊天房间里。

世界可以扩展不同城市区域，例如：

- **Star Bay** — 更安静的社交与关系氛围；
- **Cloud Studio** — 创作、事业与灵感；
- **Neon District** — 夜生活、活力与随机事件；
- **Travel Port** — 出发、旅行与远方记忆；
- 更多可持续扩展的城市区域与目的地。

地点可以承载约会、事业机会、购物、事件、记忆和多阶段剧情。

## 6. 会留下痕迹的旅行

旅行不是“换一张背景图”，而应该成为人生历史的一部分。

玩法方向包括：

- 旅行目的地；
- 旅行印章；
- 永久旅行记忆；
- 地区专属事件；
- 与角色共同发生的旅行片段；
- 稀有或季节剧情；
- 未来更多收藏和目的地解锁。

旅行结束以后，那段经历仍然属于玩家的人生。

## 7. 时间、天气与季节

世界应该有自己的时间感。

可以包含：

- 世界日期推进；
- 天气；
- 早晚阶段；
- 季节变化；
- 生日与纪念日；
- 周期事件；
- 限时机会；
- 玩家离线后的返回补算。

世界不会只有玩家点击时才“存在”，这正是连续感的重要来源。

## 8. 多阶段剧情链

AI Life 的事件可以互相连接，而不是一堆没有关系的弹窗。

今天的一个选择，未来可能变成：

- 新邀请；
- 关系变化；
- 事业机会；
- 冲突；
- 承诺；
- 新地点；
- 稀有物品；
- 永久记忆。

长期目标是让“设计好的剧情”与“玩家自己的历史”共同生成越来越个人化的人生故事。

## 9. 每天回来都有一点期待

每日体验可以包括：

- 查看离线期间发生了什么；
- 回应一个关系事件；
- 完成一个生活目标；
- 推进事业项目；
- 探索城市事件；
- 升级家庭；
- 准备一次旅行；
- 获得一段新记忆或成就。

每日系统不应该变成打卡上班，而应该制造好奇、期待和轻量成长。

## 10. 目标、成就与收藏

AI Life 既需要情感目标，也需要游戏目标。

可以包含：

- 每日目标；
- 人生里程碑；
- 成就；
- 物品收藏；
- 旅行印章；
- 关系记忆；
- 事业成就；
- 家庭升级；
- 隐藏与稀有事件；
- 长期完成目标。

玩家最终看到的不只是等级，而是**自己构建过的人生**。

## 11. 长期记忆时间线

长期记忆是 AI Life 最重要的差异化方向之一。

时间线可以记录：

- 第一次见面；
- 重要对话；
- 约会；
- 承诺；
- 冲突与和解；
- 生日；
- 旅行；
- 成就；
- 大额购买；
- 事业里程碑；
- 人生重大事件。

玩得越久，历史本身可能越有价值。

## 12. 离线世界连续性

玩家关闭游戏以后，世界不应该彻底冻结。

产品方向包括离线返回补算和持续世界模拟，让玩家回来时看到的是“世界继续过了一段时间”，而不是永远停在退出那一秒。

同时要保持公平：世界可以继续变化，但不应该因为玩家离开几天就恶意惩罚。

## 13. 更聪明的角色方向

随着基础系统成熟，未来 AI 角色可以逐步拥有：

- 更稳定的个性；
- 基于长期记忆的对话；
- 理解当前情境的反应；
- 针对不同关系的行为；
- 会变化的偏好与习惯；
- 更动态的剧情生成；
- 更安全、更可控的生成式体验。

目标不是让角色变得随机，而是让角色因为“记得更多”而更连贯。

这些属于未来路线，正式上线前不会作为当前全面可用能力宣传。

## 14. 玩家自己的身份

第二人生不仅角色要有身份，玩家也应该有长期身份。

未来可以形成：

- 个人档案与人生历史；
- 生活方式偏好；
- 关系身份；
- 事业身份；
- 家居风格；
- 收藏和成就；
- 旅行历史；
- 更丰富的人物外观和个性化。

玩得越久，玩家越不像“系统默认主角”，而更像真正属于自己的数字人生。

## 15. 未来社交世界

当安全、隐私和内容治理体系成熟后，可以扩展：

- 好友拜访；
- 共享地点；
- 社区活动；
- 协作体验；
- 世界发现；
- 可选共享记忆；
- 社区创造空间。

社交不是强制玩法。即使喜欢独自体验的玩家，也应该拥有完整的第二人生。

## 16. 创作者生态

长期世界天然适合创作者参与。

未来可以开放：

- 创作者剧情；
- 创作者设计地点；
- 时装和装饰内容；
- 角色概念；
- 活动设计；
- 旅行目的地；
- 创作者策划的人生体验。

目标是在不暴露私有引擎、安全实现的前提下，让更多创作者扩展这个世界。

## 17. 商业化空间

AI Life 可以逐步形成多种商业模式：

- 免费进入的大型基础体验；
- 可选高级剧情与目的地；
- 季节内容；
- 外观与个性化；
- 家居与生活方式内容；
- 适合时提供可选订阅；
- 创作者内容分成；
- 角色/IP 联动；
- 娱乐合作；
- 未来数字生命平台服务。

这些是商业方向，不代表已经产生对应收入。

## 18. 为什么玩家会长期回来

AI Life 同时拥有多种长期循环：

- **情感循环** — 关系与记忆；
- **成长循环** — 事业、家庭、经济与成就；
- **探索循环** — 新地点、新事件、新剧情链；
- **收藏循环** — 物品、印章、里程碑和记忆；
- **连续循环** — 世界会继续变化；
- **身份循环** — 第二人生越来越像“我的人生”；
- **期待循环** — 没完成的承诺、未来事件与长期后果。

目的不是用摩擦把用户困住，而是让用户觉得：**这个世界值得回来。**

## 19. AI Life 的核心差异

AI Life 不只是：

- 聊天机器人；
- 恋爱模拟器；
- 放置点击游戏；
- 静态视觉小说；
- 普通人生模拟器的复制品。

真正的产品组合是：

**长期关系 + 持久记忆 + 持续世界 + 人生成长 + 玩家历史。**

这才是“第二人生”的基础。
